Methodology

Potentials

Referenced to the saturated calomel electrode (SCE) in flowing seawater, drawn from ASTM G82 and MIL-STD-889. Rounded to 2 decimal places for clarity.

Severity thresholds

Conventionally tolerable below ~150 mV, aggressive above ~500 mV. Sheltered ×2.0, standard ×1.0, coastal ×0.5 — reflecting how electrolyte availability changes corrosion rate.

Limits

Does not model crevice / pitting, area ratios quantitatively, surface treatments, microclimates, or time-dependent passivation. Screening tool only.
